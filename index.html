Architectural Wonders of India

42 UNESCO World Heritage Sites preserving thousands of years of stone carving, celestial mathematics, and royal dynastic legacies.

UNESCO Inscription #252 ASI Protected
Agra, Uttar Pradesh • Yamuna Basin

The Taj Mahal & Yamuna Riverbank Complex

Commissioned in 1632 CE by Mughal Emperor Shah Jahan to honor Empress Mumtaz Mahal. Renowned globally for absolute octagonal symmetry, subterranean well foundations engineered against seismic stress, and brilliant translucent Makrana white marble.

Architectural Order Indo-Islamic Classical
Inlay Technique Pietra Dura (Parchin Kari)
Construction Era 1632 – 1653 CE
Preservation Custodian Archaeological Survey of India
1199 CE
Mehrauli, New Delhi

Qutub Minar Complex

World's tallest brick minaret at 72.5m, accompanied by the enigmatic 4th-century rust-resistant Iron Pillar of Chandragupta II.

Red Sandstone & Marble • Mamluk Dynasty
13th Cent.
Konark, Odisha

Konark Sun Temple

Colossal stone chariot of Surya carved with 24 giant wheels that act as precise astronomical solar chronometers.

Kalinga Style • Eastern Ganga Dynasty
8th Cent.
Aurangabad, Maharashtra

Kailasa Temple & Ellora

Earth's largest monolithic excavation, carved top-down from single basalt cliff, removing 200,000 tons of mountain.

Monolithic Basalt • Rashtrakuta Dynasty
15th Cent.
Ballari, Karnataka

Stone Chariot of Hampi

Shrine to Garuda in Vijaya Vittala Temple, amidst the boulder-strewn moonscape of the 14th-century Vijayanagara Empire.

Dravidian Stone • Vijayanagara Empire
1648 CE
Shahjahanabad, Old Delhi

Red Fort (Lal Qila)
